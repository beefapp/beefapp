# Beef Support

Welcome to **Beef** Support!

Thank you for using Beef. We're committed to helping you get the most out of your fitness journey. Whether you're tracking workouts, cardio, or nutrition, we're here to help if you run into any issues or have suggestions for improving the app.

## Contact Support

If you need assistance, have a question, or would like to report a bug, please contact us at:

**Email:** [beefappsupport@gmail.com](mailto:beefappsupport@gmail.com)

We strive to respond to all support requests as quickly as possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Beef?

Beef is a fitness app designed to help you track your workouts, cardio sessions, and nutrition in one place, making it easier to monitor your progress and reach your fitness goals.

### I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please send an email to **[beefappsupport@gmail.com](mailto:beefappsupport@gmail.com)** and include:

* A description of the issue
* Your iPhone model
* Your iOS version
* Screenshots or screen recordings (if applicable)
* Steps to reproduce the issue

This information helps us identify and fix problems more quickly.

### I have a feature request.

We love hearing ideas from our users! If there's a feature you'd like to see in Beef, send us an email with your suggestion. User feedback plays an important role in shaping future updates.

### My workout or nutrition data isn't appearing correctly.

If you're experiencing syncing or tracking issues, try:

* Closing and reopening the app
* Restarting your device
* Ensuring you've granted the necessary permissions (such as Health access, if enabled)
* Updating to the latest version of Beef

If the problem continues, please contact us using the support email above.
